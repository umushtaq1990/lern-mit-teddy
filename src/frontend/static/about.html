What is Lern mit Teddy?

Lern mit Teddy is a voice-based language learning companion for kids. Your child talks out loud with "Teddy," a friendly AI bear, who guides them through vocabulary and conversation practice — no reading or typing required to get started.

How it works

  • Pick a language to learn (German, English, Arabic, or Hindi) and your child's native language
  • Talk naturally — Teddy listens, replies out loud, and gently corrects mistakes
  • Teddy works through vocabulary cards one topic at a time — greetings, numbers, colors, family, and more
  • Optional: create a free login so progress and history carry across devices instead of staying on just one browser

Is it safe for kids?

  • No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing to buy
  • Logging in is optional — kids can use the app anonymously with just a first name
  • We record conversation transcripts and basic usage data (session length, language chosen) to improve how well Teddy teaches and to fix problems
  • If your child creates a login, we store their email and a stable ID so their learning history can follow them across devices
  • We don't sell or share this data with third parties
